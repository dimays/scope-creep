- **Date range:** 2026-09-04 – 2026-09-06

## Highlights
Three days that took Scope Creep from a stabilized single-user factory to a small,
self-governing organization. The Console's Threads feature was reframed to a ToS-clean
projection + launcher after a real compliance catch ([[adr-016]]); the org grew a
reusable employee/template staffing model ([[adr-017]], [[adr-020]]) under a new CEO and
chairman-of-the-board Owner posture ([[adr-018]]); the staffing-review cadence became
self-tuning and was automated as a scheduled cloud routine
([[ledger-043-staffing-cadence-self-tuning]], [[ledger-044-staffing-loop-automated]]); the
operating-loop system (dev-cycle, roadmap, evolve) was ratified and its recurring loops
scheduled ([[adr-021]], [[ledger-046-loops-scheduled]]); and a governance change moving
routine PR-merges from Owner-keystroke to independent org review was proposed,
CRO-reviewed, and held pending activation rails ([[adr-022]],
[[ledger-048-autonomous-merge-cro-and-decision]]).
