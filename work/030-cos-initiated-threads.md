Phase 2 of [[prd-cos-threads]]. Today every Owner-facing surface is Owner-initiated;
this gives the org a voice.

- The **CoS can open a thread** (or post a followup on an existing one) when it needs
  the Owner's input or wants to follow up.
- The Owner gets a **"needs-you" queue** — threads parked on him (`needs-you` state).

**Acceptance:** the CoS can start/follow-up a thread; the Owner sees a queue of threads
awaiting him, distinct from `working`/`closed`. See [[prd-cos-threads]], [[work-029]].
