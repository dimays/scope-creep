# Ledger 031 — Org-design proposals (pool + loops)

**Date:** 2026-09-05 · **Recorded by:** Chief of Staff

## Assessment (Owner-asked)
- **Pool:** the six roles are well-differentiated — the level-set dry run produced
  non-overlapping, high-signal findings and the CRO caught the CTO overstating a claim.
  **No urgent role gap.** Two *emerging* ones: **resource stewardship** (we hit the usage
  limit twice) and **reliability/ops** (latent until the Console deploys to a real URL).
- **Loops:** the leverage is in ritualizing what's currently ad hoc.

## Proposed (for Owner review)
- **[[decision-rights]]** (proposed standard) — the decision-type → required-hats map, so
  "the right people in the room" is a rule, not the CoS's ad-hoc judgment. Two invariants:
  the CRO verifies every load-bearing decision; the CoS ratifies and it is recorded.
- **[[work-033]]** — a recurring **level-set loop** (institutionalize the self-correction
  that just found the RR8 breach + the lying `/healthz`).
- **[[work-034]]** — adopt [[decision-rights]] + a **decision loop** for load-bearing calls.
- **[[work-035]]** — a **resource-stewardship** standard (model tiers, spawn discipline,
  graceful usage-limit behavior); ties to [[work-018]].

## Not done
Nothing ratified — these are drafts. The Owner reviews [[decision-rights]] and picks which
proposals to activate. Reliability/ops stays a latent CTO duty until deploy.
