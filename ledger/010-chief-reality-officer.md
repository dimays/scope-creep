# Ledger 010 — Chief Reality Officer

**Date:** 2026-09-04 · **Recorded by:** Chief of Staff

## Org change (ratified)
- **Chief Reality Officer created** as the sixth core C-suite agent ([[adr-007]]).
  Owner-proposed, CoS-ratified. Canonical charter + subagent loader; registered.
- **Mandate:** cross-check load-bearing decisions, research, and "done/works" claims
  for hallucination, invention, and unchecked assumptions — label claims verified /
  inferred / assumed, and verify the plan-breaking assumption first. Prefers a
  runnable check over an opinion, including its own.
- **Scoped** to high-stakes checkpoints (anti-ceremony, per [[adr-006]]); woven into
  [[ticket-cycle]] as a reality-check before decisions/research/done.

## Motivating evidence
The branch-protection unchecked assumption ([[ledger-009-branch-protection]]) — the
exact failure mode the CRO exists to catch.
